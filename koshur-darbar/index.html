Our story

A darbar for the flavours of home.

In Kashmir, a good meal is never a small thing. It is a gathering: a copper trami set down between friends, steam rising from the rice, and course after course carried out by the waza.

Koshur Darbar was made for that kind of meal. We cook the dishes Kashmiri families grow up with: slow braises coloured with Kashmiri chilli and cockscomb flower, yogurt gravies scented with fennel and dried mint, and meat pounded by hand until it is silky. Then we serve them the way they are meant to be eaten, together.

Koshurkọ́-shur · adj.Kashmiri, in the Kashmiri language itself. The language, the people and the food.
Darbardar-bār · n.A royal court: a place where guests are received with honour and generosity.

Wazwan Classics

The mutton and chicken dishes of a traditional Kashmiri feast.

  • Rogan Josh

    Signature

    Tender mutton braised in a deep red gravy of Kashmiri chilli, fennel and ginger, coloured with ratan jot.

  • Gushtaba

    Minced mutton with spices, shaped on skewers and cooked over coals.

The Wazwan

A feast in four acts

Wazwan is the great ceremonial meal of Kashmir, cooked by master chefs called wazas. It is shared from a single copper trami, usually by four guests, and served course by course.

It begins with Tash-t-Nari

Before the first dish arrives, a copper ewer and basin are carried to each guest for washing hands. Only then is the trami set down.

Act I

The Trami

A mound of rice, crowned with the first dishes for everyone to share.

  1. Tabak Maaz fried ribs
  2. Seekh Kabab skewers
  3. Methi Maaz fenugreek
  4. Waza Kokur chicken
Act II

The Reds

Rich, chilli-warm gravies served one after another.

  1. Rista meatballs
  2. Rogan Josh red braise
  3. Marchwangan Korma fiery
Act III

The Whites

Cooling yogurt and milk gravies, fragrant with fennel and cardamom.

  1. Daniwal Korma coriander
  2. Aab Gosht milk
  3. Yakhni yogurt
Finale

Gushtaba

The last and most celebrated course. Once Gushtaba is served, the feast is complete, and a cup of kahwa follows.

  1. Gushtaba the finale
  2. Phirni & Kahwa to close

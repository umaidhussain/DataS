# Photo & video credits

All photography on this page is real (no AI-generated or stock-illustration
images), sourced from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under licenses that permit
commercial use and modification (cropping/resizing). None of it was taken at
Koshur Darbar — it stands in for the real thing until the restaurant supplies
its own photos and video. The two background videos are motion made from two
of these real photos (a slow zoom/pan "Ken Burns" effect), not on-site footage.

Attribution is required by these licenses even though the images were cropped
and resized. Keep this file alongside `media/` if the images stay in use, and
carry the attributions into the live site's footer or an /about page.

Notes on what was deliberately left out during sourcing:
- A "Kashmiri Pulao" photo was rejected because its props included a national
  flag graphic — not appropriate for a restaurant site, especially given
  Kashmir's political context.
- Two Dal Lake photos were rejected because they show an identifiable
  person's face in close-up (a boatman) — using a recognizable person's photo
  on a commercial site without their consent is a publicity-rights concern.
- A tea photo was rejected because the disposable cup carries a visible
  third-party brand logo.

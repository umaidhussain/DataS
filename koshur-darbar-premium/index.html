About Koshur Darbar

Where every meal feels like a homecoming.

"Koshur" means Kashmiri. "Darbar" means a court of honoured guests. Together, they are a promise: come to our table, and you'll be treated like family.

Koshur Darbar was built around the Wazwan tradition — the great feast of Kashmir, where dozens of dishes are cooked slowly by hand and shared from a single copper trami. We cook the same way: real ghee, hand-pounded meat, Kashmiri chillies for colour rather than heat, and a patience that can't be rushed.

Authentic RecipesPassed down through generations of Kashmiri wazas.

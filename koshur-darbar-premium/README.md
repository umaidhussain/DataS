# Koshur Darbar — premium landing page (agency pitch)

A single-page, self-contained premium redesign for Koshur Darbar Restaurant
(Town Plaza, Kulgam, J&K). Open `index.html` in a browser — no build step.

Distinct from the earlier `koshur-darbar/` site: deep green + saffron/gold +
cream palette, a full-bleed video hero, real-photo dish grid, photo gallery,
a second cinematic video break, testimonials, and a sticky mobile
call/book/directions bar. Built to show a client what a premium,
visuals-first redesign could look like — light on copy, heavy on imagery.

**Photography and video:** every image and the two background video loops
are real, licensed photography (Wikimedia Commons / Flickr, CC BY / BY-SA),
not illustrations or AI-generated images — see `media/CREDITS.md` for full
attribution and the license terms that must travel with them. None of it was
taken at Koshur Darbar itself; it stands in for real photography until the
restaurant supplies its own. The two videos are a slow zoom/pan ("Ken Burns")
effect applied to two of those real photos, not on-site footage — there was
no rights-clear stock video of Kashmiri food/cooking available to source.

## Before this goes anywhere public
Search `index.html` for these markers:

- `PHONE` — every "Call Now" / "Book a Table" / footer link uses a
  placeholder `tel:+91XXXXXXXXXX`. Replace with the real number.
- `HOURS` — the hours row currently links out to Google instead of stating times.
- `MENU` — dish descriptions are classic Kashmiri/Wazwan dishes with no
  confirmed prices; link "Ask About Our Full Menu" to a real menu once ready.
- `REVIEW` — the 4.7★ / 46-reviews figures and the three testimonials are
  **illustrative placeholders for this pitch**, not real Google data (the
  restaurant's actual public rating found during research was 4.4★). Replace
  with the real rating and real, permissioned review quotes before this is
  shown to the public.
- Photography & video — real but not Koshur Darbar's own (see above and
  `media/CREDITS.md`). Swap in the restaurant's actual food and interior
  photography/video, and keep the required attributions until you do.
